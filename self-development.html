فن اللامبالاه

يقدّم الكتاب فكرة أن السعادة الحقيقية تأتي من تقبّل الألم والمسؤولية، واختيار ما يستحق اهتمامك فعلًا، بدل محاولة إرضاء الجميع. ويؤكد أن اللامبالاة ليست تجاهلًا للحياة، بل تركيز على الأهم والتخلي عن التوقعات غير الواقعية.

The 7 Habits Of Highly Effective People

The book teaches seven core habits for personal and professional success: taking responsibility for your actions, planning with clear goals, focusing on important tasks, creating win-win solutions, understanding others before seeking to be understood, working collaboratively, and continually improving yourself.

The Power Of Positive Thinking

The book teaches that maintaining a positive mindset, trusting yourself, and having faith can reduce stress, build confidence, and transform your life. Through practical steps and affirmations, it shows how positive thoughts lead to better decisions, stronger relationships, and greater success.
